Buy the lot. Read the clues. Pack the haul.

Lot & Haul is a single-player strategy/management game about storage-lot auctions, suspicious treasures, and the expensive little voice that says, "I think this is worth something."

Every run starts with a choice: which lot looks promising, how much energy to spend inspecting it, and how far you are willing to bid before you really know what is inside. A dusty object might be junk, a damaged collectible, a fake, or the quiet score that pays for your next upgrade. You will rarely have the full truth before the auction ends. That is the game.

Win a lot, squeeze the best finds into your vehicle, bring them home, and decide what deserves repair, restoration, or authentication. Then sell to nightly customers who want specific kinds of items, fit those items into their car grids, and choose whether to take the safe offer or push your luck for a better deal.

What You Do In The Alpha

  • Browse storage lots across different locations and decide which ones are worth your time.
  • Spend limited inspection points to reveal clues before you bid.
  • Compete in auctions where every extra bid might turn a bargain into a mistake.
  • Pack won items into your vehicle's cargo grid, because space is part of the cost.
  • Bring items home to storage, where more clues are revealed and research actions can change what you know.
  • Repair, restore, or authenticate items to improve condition or uncover hidden value.
  • Sell to nightly customers by matching demand tags, packing their cars, and choosing conservative or aggressive sale strategies.
  • Use your earnings to improve attributes, buy vehicles, and prepare for better hauls.

Why It Gets Risky

Appraised value is not the whole truth. Surface clues can make an item look promising, but authentication may reveal a hidden flaw or a hidden jackpot.

Inspection is a bet before the bid. Spend too little and you may buy trash. Spend too much and you may waste your best chance on the wrong lot.

Cargo space matters. Winning is only useful if you can take the good stuff home.

Selling is not a cash-out button. Customers have tastes, cars have shapes, and the aggressive sale option can make a good flip better or worse.

Current Alpha

This is an early public alpha focused on proving the core loop: inspect, bid, haul, research, sell, upgrade, repeat. The main shape of the game is playable, but the build is still rough.

Expect placeholder and AI-assisted visuals, provisional balance, incomplete onboarding, limited content density, and UI that still needs polish. If something is confusing, slow, too easy, too punishing, or weirdly satisfying, that is exactly the kind of feedback this release is looking for.

The Most Helpful Feedback

  • Where did you first feel confused?
  • Did inspection give you enough information to make an interesting bid?
  • Did the difference between appraised value and verified value feel exciting, frustrating, or unclear?
  • Did cargo packing and customer selling feel like strategy, friction, or both?
  • Which rough edge most hurt the playtest: UI clarity, balance, pacing, art, tutorial, or bugs?
  • What was the best decision you made during a run?

Near-Term Roadmap

These are the player-facing improvements I expect to focus on next:

  • Clearer shared item cards for inspection, storage, cargo, and selling.
  • Better clue presentation so unknowns, revealed information, condition, and value are easier to understand at a glance.
  • More readable onboarding for the full run flow.
  • Balance passes for prices, inspection costs, customer demand, attributes, and vehicle progression.
  • More coherent item, vehicle, warehouse, and location art to replace the roughest placeholders.
  • Quality-of-life tools that reduce repeated clicking during playtesting, with possible player-facing helpers later if they stay fun.

Larger ideas for later include garage-sale style buying, recurring customers, rival bidders, expert contacts, calendar events, collection/prestige goals, and deeper vehicle identity. Those are directions, not promises for this alpha.

Build Notes

This first public alpha is meant for playtest feedback. Saves, balance, item data, and progression may change between builds.
